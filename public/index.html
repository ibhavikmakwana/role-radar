          OPEN-SOURCE AGENT ECOSYSTEM
          
CareerOps AI Job Search Agent

        

        
      

      

        
"Apply better to fewer. Signal over volume. Evidence over keywords. A human decides. Local-first. Dignity on both sides of the table."

        
        
Tech Roles Radar incorporates the official methodology of career-ops — the open-source AI job search agent created by Santiago Fernández de Valderrama Aparicio (@santifer):

        
        
    
          
  • A–H Evaluation Framework: Multi-block candidate-to-job matching, gap analysis, and level strategy.
    • 
          
  • 1–5 Scoring Discipline: Recommends applying only to ≥4.0 roles, stopping you from wasting nights on weak fits.
    • 
          
  • ATS Cover Letters & CV Bullets: Research-backed tailored assets with zero keyword fabrication.
    • 
          
  • Local-First Privacy: No telemetry, no backend resume upload. Your data stays on your machine.
    • 
          
  • Seamless CLI Export: Export your pipeline directly into data/applications.md for the CareerOps CLI.
    • 
        


        

          
RUN CAREEROPS LOCALLY IN YOUR AI CLI:

          
npx @santifer/career-ops init
